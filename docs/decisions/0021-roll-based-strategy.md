# 0021 Roll-based strategy: Adjust+Swap

Status: proposed, for the owner's review. Resolves part of the recorded gap "a roll-based strategy" (decision 0011 item 1; decision 0009 listed "roll or swap recommendation" as out of scope). Source: the owner's file `Roll-based_decision_strategy.md`. This record covers **Adjust+Swap** only; **Close-out+New** is the next change.

## Owner answers (asked in chat)
1. **Roll inputs** (near date, far date, offset) are **entered per cycle**. The far date defaults to the policy's tenor date (with calendars) and the offset to a policy default. They live in Hedge Recommendation; Exposure Management is unchanged.
2. **Several maturing value dates**: separate single-leg forwards (or NDFs) **tied together**, not an instrument SWAP. One close-out per value date and one far trade. FX Trade's swap shape is unchanged.
3. **Scope**: Adjust+Swap first.
4. **Window**: from the near date to the near date plus the offset. (The file says "far leg to far leg plus offset" once; the owner confirmed it is the near leg.)
5. **Deciding**: the trades are accepted or dismissed **as one package**. An override changes only the adjustment amount (the combined far trade when they are booked as one); the close-outs and the rolled amount are what is maturing.
6. **No roll dates for a cycle**: no action, with a reason. Every group is `NONE` with `ROLL_DATES_NOT_SET`.

## Decision
- **Policy.** `rollType` (`NONE` default, or `ADJUST_SWAP`) sits beside the strategy: the ratio or amount-threshold rule still decides the adjustment, and the roll is added to it. `rollAdjustment` is `SEPARATE` (default: close-outs, the roll and the adjustment are separate trades) or `COMBINED` (the roll and the adjustment are one trade at the far date, as the file's two-trade form). `rollOffsetDays` (default 0) is the offset used when a cycle gives none.
- **Roll dates.** `setRollDates` (permission `hedge-policy:manage`) enters near date, optional far date and optional offset for a cycle. It is allowed only while the program rolls and before the exposure is approved; the near date must be after the as-of date and the far date after near plus offset. The user then reruns the cycle. A change goes to the audit trail. A group whose far date (entered, or the policy's tenor date) is not after the window gets `ROLL_DATES_NOT_SET` too, so a roll never ends before it starts.
- **What matures.** Exposure Analysis now keeps, on each hedge row, its value date, the net group it fell in and its amount in that group's exposure currency (migration 0007; rows of earlier runs have none and are ignored). Hedge Recommendation takes each group's legs with a value date from the near date to near plus offset, both included.
- **The trades.** Legs are netted by value date (signed; a hedge against a long exposure is negative). Each date that nets to something gets a **close-out** at that date in the opposite direction (a sold hedge is closed by buying). The **roll** puts back the total at the far date, in the hedges' direction. The **adjustment** is the existing rule's amount at the far date. Nothing maturing gives the plain adjustment at the far date; no adjustment gives the roll trades only (`reason` says why, for example `WITHIN_TARGET`); dates that offset each other are closed with no roll. Rounding, the minimum and the maximum/split apply to the adjustment only; the close-outs and the roll are exact.
- **One far trade.** In `COMBINED` the far trade is the signed sum of roll and adjustment: smaller or larger the other way it shrinks or flips, and exactly offsetting means no far trade, only close-outs. It is not split by the maximum.
- **Parts.** One recommendation per group, as before. Its parts are numbered close-outs (by value date), roll, then adjustment parts, and carry a `role`. Close-outs and the roll carry their own direction, value date and fixing date; the recommendation's direction, amount, value date and fixing date are those of the adjustment (of the combined trade). NDF fixing dates use the existing calendar rule. Accepting makes one pre-trade per part in one all-or-none FX Trade call, as for a split; the **recommendation ID is the roll ID** that ties them.
- **Override.** Replaces only the adjustment (or the combined trade) and is split as before; the other parts are kept. A recommendation with no adjustment cannot be overridden (`there is no adjustment to override`); it can be accepted or dismissed.
- **Record.** A rolled recommendation keeps its roll type, mode, near date, far date and offset as they were when it was made.

## Changes from review of PR 14
Owner answers: **gate Approve Exposure**; **show the closed far date, the user re-enters another**.
1. **Dates are locked to the approved run.** Roll dates carry a `revision`; each recommendation keeps the revision it was made from. Exposure Management asks Hedge Recommendation before it approves a run (`DecisionsPort.checkExposureApproval`, the way Approve Cycle already does for decision-makers). For a rolling program it refuses unless the run's recommendations exist (a run with no groups has none and passes) and were all made from the dates as they now stand; otherwise: "rerun the cycle before approving the exposure". When it agrees it **locks the dates for that run** (`roll_locks`) until the approval commits; a refused or failed approval unlocks them (`releaseExposureCheck`), and a rerun makes a lock for the old run moot. Editing the dates and the check take the same per-cycle lock as decisions, so one is wholly before the other: an edit first makes the approval refuse; the check first makes the edit refuse. Recommendations are made under that lock with the dates read inside it, so a delayed "run finished" event cannot attach dates that changed after the approval looked (they cannot change once it has). The approval refuses if the cycle was rerun while it was being checked. Last guard: a decision (accept or override) is refused if the dates' revision is not the one the recommendation was made from.
2. **The entity that holds the hedge books its own close-out.** Close-outs are netted per value date **and holding entity**, never across entities. Each close-out part carries its entity; the roll, the adjustment and the combined far trade are booked by the group's entity (the netting level's own, else the policy's default). Booked as one far trade, the close-outs are unchanged. With no trading entity for the far trades the package is `NO_TRADING_ENTITY`; a package of close-outs alone needs none.
3. **A closed far date is shown, not moved.** An entered far date that is closed in any currency of a group's pair (its calendar, weekends included) gives that group no recommendation and no trade: reason `FAR_DATE_CLOSED`, with the date, the closed currencies and the next joint business day (`rollIssue`). The user enters the date they choose in the roll dates and reruns; one far date per cycle. A defaulted far date (the tenor date) is still rolled modified following, as before.

## Changes from the second review of PR 14
Two blockers, both real.
1. **A closed far date keeps the cycle editable.** Approve Exposure is refused while any group on the current run has `FAR_DATE_CLOSED` ("enter another far date and rerun the cycle before approving the exposure"). The refusal happens before any lock is taken, so the cycle is not frozen: the user enters a date valid for the affected pairs, reruns and approves. The date is never moved for them.
2. **A lock belongs to one approval attempt.** Each Approve Exposure attempt makes an `attemptId`; the gate's lock row is keyed by it (migration `0011_roll_lock_owner`) and `releaseExposureCheck` deletes only that attempt's row. A refused attempt (segregation of duties, say) therefore cannot release the lock of another attempt that has passed the gate and not yet committed. A date edit still refuses while any lock exists for the current run. The two services keep separate transactions: the gate commits its own lock, and Exposure Management's approval commits on its own.
Tests: refusal while closed, then a valid date, rerun, approval; and a deterministic pause of a valid attempt before commit, a competing refused attempt and its cleanup, a date edit still refused, then the commit with the dates unchanged. Mutations (closed date allowed, release not fenced to the attempt, the gate clearing other attempts' locks) each fail a test.

## Changes from the third review of PR 14
A delayed "run finished" event could make recommendations from dates entered after the run finished, and the revision check would then match. Fixed by binding the dates to the run:
- When the roll dates are edited and the cycle's current run has **finished**, the edit first saves the dates that run finished with (`roll_run_bindings`, migration `0012`; none if none were set), under the cycle lock. The first edit after the run keeps them; later edits do not replace them.
- Recommendations for a run use its bound dates if it has any, else the dates now in force, and carry that revision. So a late event for run R makes R's recommendations from the dates R finished with, the approval sees they are not the current revision and refuses ("rerun the cycle"), and a decision on them is refused too. A fresh run uses the edited dates and can be approved.
- The edit reads the current run inside the cycle lock, so it is wholly before or after any other step on the cycle. No transaction spans two services.
Test: delivery to Hedge Recommendation is held, the far date is edited twice, delivery is released; R's recommendations show the old date, approval and decision are refused, a fresh run takes the last date and is approved. Mutations (binding ignored, not recorded, overwritten by a second edit) each fail it.

## Defaults I chose, to confirm or change
1. **The recommendation ID is the roll ID**, so FX Trade gets no new column (the earlier plan was a `roll_group_id`). The link already exists, is made together and is read back by FX Trade. Change it if you want a roll ID that groups several recommendations, which is not needed while a roll is one group.
2. **The maximum hedge applies to the adjustment only**, and not at all when roll and adjustment are one trade. The roll is existing hedge, not new.
3. **Close-outs are booked at each maturing value date** and the far legs at the far date entered. An entered far date is never moved (point 3 above); a defaulted one is rolled modified following as the tenor date is.
4. **A group with no trading entity** (at levels without an entity, no default entity in the policy) gets `NO_TRADING_ENTITY` when the package has a roll, adjustment or combined trade.
5. **Missing spot rate** (needed for the minimum, maximum or threshold) stops only the adjustment (`NO_RATE`); the roll trades need no rate and are still made.
6. **Offsetting dates** (one date sold, another bought) are closed and nothing is rolled.

## Tests
Pure: the window (both ends, offset 0), netting by date, direction, offsetting dates, nothing maturing, combined sums (same side, smaller, exact, flipped), date and policy checks. Exposure Analysis: each leg's value date, group and amount, adding up to the group's hedge. End to end: no dates; separate trades from accept to pre-trades; override changing only the adjustment, with a split after the roll; combined (shrunk, removed, flipped, overridden); roll only (accepted, dismissed, not overridable); empty window, tenor default and a tenor inside the window; refusals (no roll, too early, after exposure approval). Review: a date edit between run and approval, the approval looking first and the edit looking first, both at once (exactly one wins and an approved run keeps its dates), a refused approval not leaving the lock, the last-guard decision check, per-entity close-outs in separate and combined mode with the pre-trades booked by those entities, no entity, a holiday in only EUR or only USD, and the user-entered date. Mutations (window end exclusive, close-out direction, combined sign, override rewriting every part, missing dates treated as an adjustment, close-outs at the far date, split in combined mode, placement in the wrong currency or group) each fail a test.
