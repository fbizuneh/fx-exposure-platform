# 0020 Amount-threshold strategy

Status: proposed, for the owner's review. Resolves the recorded gap "amount-threshold strategy" (decision 0011 item 1). Owner instruction: a default threshold per program in the display currency, overrides by netting dimension, hedge the full net when it is larger (at or above, see below), then the same rounding, minimum and maximum/split; and keep those shared across threshold types. Owner answers: overrides by **netting level only**; a net **exactly at** the threshold is hedged (asked in chat after a review comment said so; the first description said "larger than").

## Decision
- **Strategy.** A program's policy has a `strategy`: `hedge-ratio-adjustment` (the default, as before) or `amount-threshold`. One per program. Each recommendation records the strategy that made it.
- **Threshold.** `amountThresholdDisplay`, in the display currency and above 0, plus `amountThresholdOverrides`: one per netting level, replacing the program's for groups at that level (the same shape as the maximum, decision 0016). The amount-threshold strategy needs the program threshold; the database refuses a policy that uses the strategy without one.
- **The rule.** The threshold is turned into the exposure currency at the spot rate (as the minimum and maximum are). The **net exposure** (gross plus outstanding hedges, in the exposure currency) is compared with it: **at or above it**, either way, means hedge **all of the net** (a long net is sold, a short net bought), which brings the net to zero; only a net strictly below it is within it (`WITHIN_THRESHOLD`). At a threshold of 100,000, +100,000 is sold and -100,000 bought, subject to the shared rounding, minimum and split. The comparison uses the converted threshold **unrounded**; the four-place figure is only what is stored and shown, so rounding can never move a net across the threshold (review of this PR). Without the rate the threshold cannot be compared, and the group is `NO_RATE`.
- **Shared rules.** The strategy only says what amount it wants (`ratioAdjustmentNeed`, `amountThresholdNeed`). One function, `finishRecommendation`, then rounds it down to the step, tests the minimum and picks the direction; `splitAmount` splits an amount above the maximum. A later strategy (value at risk) adds its own need function and reuses both. The ratio strategy's results are unchanged (its existing tests pass untouched).
- **One snapshot.** The policy and its overrides are read in one repeatable-read transaction, so a save committed in the middle of a read cannot give the old default with the new overrides; a save locks the policy row first, so a concurrent save waits and its "before" is whole (review of this PR).
- **What a recommendation shows.** For this strategy `ratio` is empty (it has none) and `thresholdExposureAmount` holds the threshold it was compared with; the target is what is hedged plus the whole net, and the needed amount is minus the net. For the ratio strategy the threshold field is empty.

## What changed
Migration `0008_amount_threshold`: `strategy` and `amount_threshold_display` on the policy, the override table (with a key to its policy that carries the program, decision 0018), `ratio` allowed empty and `threshold_exposure_amount` on recommendations. The contract gains the policy fields, `WITHIN_THRESHOLD`, the `amount-threshold` strategy and the two recommendation fields (`ratio` may now be null).

## Tests
Pure: the rule both ways, at the threshold and a cent either side, a hedge already in place, no rate, and the shared rounding and minimum giving the same result for both strategies; policy checks. Database: hedged in full with the threshold shown, at the threshold hedged and a cent more of threshold within, a threshold whose four-place display equals the net compared unrounded (above and below), the level override against the program default and another level ignored, rounding, minimum and both splits applied to this strategy, accepting it makes the pre-trade, read-back, a policy without a threshold refused and the database constraint, a cycle recalculated after an executed hedge deciding on the net rather than the gross, and a save committed in the middle of a policy read not being half seen. Mutations (strict instead of at-or-above, rounding before comparing, no level override, no rounding, wrong sign, gross for net, no snapshot) each fail a test.

## Not done, and why (for you to decide)
- **A threshold below the minimum hedge amount** is allowed; the minimum then decides (a net above the threshold but below the minimum is `BELOW_MINIMUM`). Refusing it, as the maximum is refused below the minimum, is a one-line change if you prefer.
- **Hedging only the excess over the threshold** (net minus threshold) rather than the full net. You asked for the full net, so that is what it does.
- **Overrides by pair, entity or region**: not built (answer above).
- **A different strategy per netting level** in one program: not built; one strategy per program.
- **A screen or API for choosing the strategy**: the policy is saved through the existing command; no UI exists yet.
